# 2026 MLB Fantasy Projections

**[Interactive Rankings App](https://mgfantasyranks2026.streamlit.app/)**

A machine learning approach to fantasy baseball player valuation.

## Overview

This book documents the methodology behind building ML-based fantasy point projections for the 2026 MLB season, along with actionable draft targets and fades for each position.

## Sections

**Part 1: Methodology**
- How the models were built, from data collection through final predictions

**Part 2: 2026 Picks**
- Position-by-position analysis of players to target or avoid. Based on both model predictions and other analysis
