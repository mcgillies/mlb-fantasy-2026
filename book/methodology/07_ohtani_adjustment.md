# How to handle Ohtani

For whatever reason ESPN does not allow for the splitting of Shohei into two separate players, one hitter and one pitcher. As is he is simply too overpowered; ESPN projects him for 911 fantasy points with Judge the second highest at 577. Here I propose a way to handle this. 

## Assumptions:
- The aim will be to value Ohtani at around 650 projected points, making him the #1 overall pick by ~50 fpoints still but reducing the gap. 
- He will pitch on average 1 out of every 8 days. 
- On days he pitches, he will not be able to accumulate fantasy points from batting. 
- Replacement level player will produce ~280 fantasy points. 


ESPN projects Ohtani to have 537 fantasy points from batting, therefore 374 from pitching. Following the assumption Ohtani pitches once every 8 days, we adjust the projected batting fantasy points to 537 * (7/8) = ~470, with the total now at 844. With the goal value of 650, we need to account for an extra 194 fantasy points somehow. So to balance things out statistically the Ohtani drafter would have to forfeit a draft pick where they would be getting someone projected ~499 fantasy points... which would be an end of first round pick, which the Ohtani drafter would not have. Therefore taking two picks and aggregating is likely easier. Third round picks will have value around 400 fantasy points, accounting for 120 fantasy points above replacement. Seventh round picks sit around a value of 350 fantasy points, accounting for 70 fantasy points above replacement. So stripping third and seventh round picks seems fair - making up the extra Ohtani value. 


## Guidance - strip the 3rd and 7th round picks of the Ohtani drafter. 
My league will enforce mandatory drafting of Dick Lovelady and Paul Dejong at these spots.
